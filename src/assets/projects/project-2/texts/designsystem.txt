The collection was rebuilt as a field archive. I added search, sorting, and grid controls so users could find previous scans by name, date, or tags, then switch the view depending on how much visual detail they needed. The detector gives live magnetic readings with a simple status system, so users can understand the result before reading raw sensor data. The statistics page was reduced from a stacked dashboard into a quick-reading summary. Instead of showing every metric at once, the redesign prioritizes the core scan count, rock categories, and average hardness, making the data easier to read at a glance during fieldwork.
