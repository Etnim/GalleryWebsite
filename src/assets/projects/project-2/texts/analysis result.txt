Result screen begins with a clear identification summary: the rock name is presented as the dominant title, while the specimen photo is reduced in size because the user has already seen it throughout the scanning flow. A colour-coded confidence percentage makes the accuracy of the identification immediately visible, and compact tags surface the most useful field data at a glance, including classification, Mohs hardness, and rarity. Below this summary, the Description and Value switcher separates geological information from market and collector insights.
